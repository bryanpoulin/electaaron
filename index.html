Five priorities · One goal

A practical voice for a stronger Ward 7.

Aaron’s priorities start with the fundamentals: dependable services, responsible decisions, strong local communities and an open line to City Hall.

01

Fix the fundamentals

Plan for roads, bridges, water, wastewater and drainage before problems become more expensive.

02

Responsible taxes & spending

Set clear priorities, ask what new commitments will cost over time, and focus on value for taxpayers.

03

Stronger Ward 7 communities

Make sure Garson, Falconbridge, Capreol, Skead and Kukagami receive fair consideration for services and facilities.

04

Local jobs & opportunity

Help local employers, entrepreneurs and organizations create more opportunities close to home.

05

Open, accessible government

Residents deserve timely answers, clear information and meaningful ways to take part in decisions.

The bigger picture

Grow opportunity. Respect the taxpayer.

A stronger local economy can widen the tax base and help support the services our communities rely on. Aaron wants Council to weigh the need, cost and long-term impact of decisions—and communicate those choices clearly.

Candidate information

Aaron Walsh

Aaron’s career spans business, communications, employment services and education. He has founded and operated businesses, worked as a business consultant supporting local entrepreneurs, managed with the Canadian Federation of Independent Business, and taught part-time at Cambrian College.

He studied political science and communications at Laurentian University, public relations at Cambrian College, and earned an executive master’s degree from Quantic School of Business and Technology. He has also served community and charitable organizations, including Inner City Home of Sudbury Food Bank and Habitat Boreal.

Aaron and his wife have been married for more than 27 years and have three daughters.
